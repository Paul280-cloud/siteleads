# SiteLeads

**One-line pitch:** SiteLeads is a lead pipeline for freelance web designers and small agencies in South Africa, turning local businesses without a website into a tracked, assignable sales pipeline.

## The problem

Freelance web developers and small digital agencies need a steady stream of new clients, but finding them is slow and unorganized. Today it means manually searching Google Maps, clicking into each business, checking if it has a website, writing the details into a spreadsheet, and hoping nobody on the team calls the same business twice.

This costs a solo freelancer or small agency hours every week — time that should go into building sites and closing deals, not hunting for leads. A small agency with two or three people calling leads has no shared view of who called whom, what was said, or what the outcome was.

## The customer

The paying customer is the **agency owner or freelance web designer**, not the local businesses being called. They are the ones who need a reliable pipeline and a way to manage a small sales team (even a team of one).

The businesses being called are the leads inside the system — small, local businesses (plumbers, salons, spaza shops, mechanics, tutors) that don't yet have a website.

## The solution

SiteLeads finds local businesses without a website, turns each one into a lead card with contact details, and lets an agency team work that lead through a simple pipeline: new → contacted → interested → converted → not interested. An agency owner manages the team and assigns leads; callers work only the leads assigned to them and log outcomes as they go.

## The impact

If this works, a freelance web designer or small agency spends less time hunting for prospects and more time delivering paid work. Success looks like: fewer duplicate calls to the same business, a visible conversion rate (leads called → sites sold), and a shorter time from "found the lead" to "signed the client."

Measurable: leads processed per week, call-to-conversion rate, and average days from lead creation to a converted client.

## The business model

Monthly subscription per agency, tiered by number of leads per month and number of caller seats:
- **Solo** — one user, a limited number of leads per month, for a freelancer working alone.
- **Team** — multiple caller seats, shared lead pool, assignment and reporting, for a small agency.

## Go-to-market

First 100 customers through:
- Freelance web developer communities and Facebook/WhatsApp groups for SA devs and agencies.
- Direct outreach to small digital agencies already doing this manually, pitched as "stop using a spreadsheet."
- A free tier or trial with a small lead quota, to get agencies to try it before paying.
- My own first real case study: N D Locksmith, a real local business without a website, used as the first proof that the pipeline finds and converts a genuine lead.

## The competition

- **BuiltWith / Wappalyzer** — detect what technology a website uses, not built for finding businesses with *no* website, and not a sales pipeline tool.
- **Manual Google Maps + spreadsheet** — what most freelancers do today; no shared pipeline, no assignment, no history.
- **General CRMs (HubSpot free tier, etc.)** — powerful but generic; not built around the specific workflow of "find a business with no website, call it, track the outcome."

SiteLeads is better because it's purpose-built for this one workflow end to end: lead discovery filtered to "no website," team assignment, and a lightweight pipeline — nothing a freelancer or small agency has to configure or adapt from a generic tool.

## Must features (day one)

1. Log in with two roles: Agency Owner (admin) and Caller.
2. Lead list showing local businesses with no website (name, category, area, phone, status).
3. Agency Owner can assign a lead to a Caller.
4. Caller sees only their assigned leads and updates the status (contacted / interested / converted / not interested) with a note.
5. Agency Owner dashboard: leads by status, conversion rate, leads per caller.
