# Loom script — SiteLeads (target: 4:30–5:00)

Record your screen with audio. Have the live site (siteleads.vercel.app) open, logged in as the owner, with a caller account ready to switch to. Read this loosely — don't sound robotic, but hit every beat.

## 1. The pitch (0:00–0:45)

> "Hi, I'm Bheki, and this is SiteLeads.
>
> Web design agencies and freelancers make money by finding local businesses that don't have a website yet, and pitching them one. The problem is finding those businesses takes hours of manual Googling, and once you've got a list, it's easy to lose track of who you've actually called and what they said.
>
> SiteLeads fixes that. It's a lead pipeline: an agency owner adds leads, assigns them to callers on their team, and tracks every one from 'new' through to a signed deal — all in one place instead of a messy spreadsheet."

## 2. Sign up / log in (0:45–1:15)

- Show the welcome screen, click **Sign up**.
- "Anyone can create an Agency Owner account — it creates their own agency automatically."
- Log in, land on `/leads`.

## 3. Lead list — add + assign (1:15–2:15)

- "Here's the leads screen. I can add a new lead with a name, category, area and phone number."
- Add one live on screen.
- "Once I've got leads, I assign them to a caller on my team right from this dropdown."
- Assign the new lead to your demo caller.
- "And I can filter by status — new, contacted, interested, converted, or not interested."
- Demo the filter dropdown.

## 4. Owner dashboard (2:15–2:45)

- Click **Dashboard**.
- "This gives the owner a quick view: total leads, conversion rate, how many were added this week, a breakdown by status, and — this is the part agency owners actually care about — how many leads each caller has worked and converted."

## 5. Map view (2:45–3:15)

- Click **Map**.
- "Leads can also have a location, and I built a map view so an owner can see at a glance where their leads are, color-coded by status. Click a pin and you get the details."

## 6. Switch to the caller (3:15–4:15)

- Log out, log back in as the demo caller.
- "Now I'm logged in as a caller. I only see leads assigned to me — not the whole agency's list, that's enforced at the database level, not just hidden in the UI."
- Click into a lead.
- "I update the status after I've made the call, add a note about what happened, and save — and that instantly reflects back on the owner's dashboard."
- Demo a status change + note.

## 7. Invite a caller (4:15–4:35)

- Log back in as owner, click **Invite a caller**.
- "Owners invite callers directly — callers can't sign themselves up, so an agency never ends up with the wrong person seeing their leads."

## 8. Close (4:35–5:00)

> "SiteLeads is built with React, TypeScript and Supabase, fully tested, and deployed live at siteleads.vercel.app. Thanks for watching."

---

**Before recording:** make sure the demo caller account has at least one assigned lead already, so step 6 doesn't start from an empty state.
